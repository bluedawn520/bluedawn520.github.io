## 经济类 Unit 1

### 文章主题

反向抵押贷款（Reverse Mortgage）—— 本应帮助老年人居有定所的贷款，却在很多情况下反而使他们居无定所。

### 长难句解析

**Sentence 1**

> The very loans that are supposed to help seniors stay in their homes are in many cases pushing them out.

- **主干**：The very loans are pushing them out
- **that** 引导定语从句修饰 loans
- **be supposed to**：主语为"人"时意为"应该"；主语为"物"时意为"本应"
- **翻译**：那些本应该帮助老年人居有定所的贷款，在很多情况下却使得他们居无定所。

**Sentence 2**

> Reverse mortgages, which allow homeowners 62 and older to borrow money against the value of their homes and not pay it back until they move out or die, have long been fraught with problems.

- **主干**：Reverse mortgages have long been fraught with problems
- **which** 引导非限制性定语从句
- **borrow** 和 **pay** 并列
- **翻译**：反向抵押贷款允许 62 岁及以上的屋主以房子作抵押来借钱，直到他们搬走或去世再偿还。这种方式一直问题重重。

**Sentence 3**

> But federal and state regulators are documenting new instances of abuse as smaller mortgage brokers, including former subprime lenders, flood the market after the recent exit of big banks and as defaults on the loans hit record rates.

- **主干**：regulators are documenting new instances of abuse
- **as** 引导时间状语从句（两个并列）
- **including** 插入语修饰 brokers
- **翻译**：随着一些业务规模较小的抵押代理人（包括之前的次贷方）在大银行退出后涌入市场，以及贷款拖欠屡创新高，联邦和各州调控机构正在记录新的滥用案例。

**Sentence 4**

> Some widows are facing eviction after they say they were pressured to keep their name off the deed without being told that they could be left facing foreclosure after their husbands died.

- **主干**：Some widows are facing eviction
- **after** + 宾语从句作状语
- **keep … off …** 使…不在…上
- **foreclosure** 丧失抵押品赎回权
- **翻译**：一些寡妇称，当初她们被迫不让自己的名字出现在房产契约上，但并未被告知如果丈夫离世她们将面临丧失抵押品赎回权，现在她们正面临被驱逐的困境。
