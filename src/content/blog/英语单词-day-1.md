## List 19

---

### Page 356–357

**interim** *adj.* 临时的，暂时的
- inter- 两者之间 + rim- 边 → "在两者之间擦边而过，停留片刻"
- 例：USFWS also set an **interim** goal of restoring prairie chicken populations…

**jury** *n.* 陪审团
- jur- 法律；公正 + -ry 集合名词后缀

**line-up** *n.* 队列，阵容；一组（人）
- line 线、排 + up 上 → "一个接一个排上"

**medicaid** *n.* 医疗救助计划
- medic- 医学 + aid 援助

**persuasion** *n.* 劝告，说服；信仰
- 例：Relying on ethical **persuasion** rather than law…

**address** *v.* 对付，处理；*n.* 地址；演讲
- 例：…thus **addressing** a long-standing complaint…

**revelation** *n.* 揭露；启示
- revel- = reveal- 揭露
- 例：One of the astonishing **revelations** was how little she knew…

**inherent / inherently** *adj./adv.* 内在的，固有的
- in- 在内 + her- 粘附 + -ent 后缀 → "骨子里就有的"

**inherit** *v.* 继承（传统）；遗传得到

**scattered** *adj.* 分散的，散布的
- scat- = sect- 切，割
- 例：The goals of prize-givers seem as **scattered** as criticism.

**successive** *adj.* 连续的，接连的
- 例：But **successive** governments have presided over selling green spaces…

**preside over** 主持；管理
**squeeze** *v.* 压榨
**decline** *v.* 下降

### Page 358–359

**upstart** *n.* 暴发户；自命不凡的人
- up- 向上 + start 开始 → "开始向上的人"
- 例：…these **upstart** entrepreneurs cannot buy their prizes the prestige of Nobels.

**entrepreneur** *n.* 创业者，企业家
**prestige** *n.* 声望
**curb** *v.* 限制
**about-face** *n.* （态度）彻底改变，大转变
**pool** *v.* 集中资源；联合投资

**soul-crashingly hard** 心力交瘁
**dampen** *v.* 抑制，降低（热情）
- 例：…the very things that in the moment **dampen** our moods can later be sources of intense gratification.

**day-to-day experience** 日常生活

### Page 360–361

**point** *n.* 点；要点
- miss the point 没领会要点
- beside the point 不相关
- selling point 卖点

**irrelevant** *adj.* 不相干的
- ir- 否定 + relevant 有关的

**pointless** *adj.* 无意义的
- point 要点 + -less 否定 → "说不到点子上"

**community** *n.* 社区；团体；界
- commun- = common 公有的 + -ity 名词后缀
- 例：The astronomy **community** is making compromises…

**compromise** *n./v.* 妥协；让步

**issue** *v.* 发布，发行；*n.* 问题
- 例：G.I. is just a military abbreviation meaning Government **Issue**…

**abbreviation** *n.* 缩写词
**hand out** 分发
**hand in** 上交
**hand over** 移交
**hand in hand** 手拉手

### Page 362–363

**doom** *n.* 厄运；毁灭 *v.* 注定
- 例：…could avoid the forecasted **doom**…

**policy / policymaker** *n.* 政策；决策者
- pol- = pole 棒 + -cy = country → "国家的指挥棒"

**compare / comparison** *v./n.* 比较
- 例：But a **comparative** study of linguistic traits…

**species** *n.* 种类；物种
- group-living species 群居物种

**art / artist / artistic / artwork** 艺术；艺术家；艺术性的；艺术品
- anti-happy art 反对快乐的艺术

### Page 364–365

**pool** *n.* 共用物；共同储金
**arthritis** *n.* 关节炎
- arthr- 关节 + -itis 病

**popular / popularity / popularize** 流行的；普及；使流行
- 例：…the **popularity** of the republican left…

### Page 366–367

**compete / competition / competitive / competitor** 竞争；竞赛；好竞争的；竞争者

- fierce competition 激烈的竞争

**mobile / mobilize** *adj./v.* 可移动的；动员
- 例：…police can search the contents of a **mobile** phone without a warrant…

**complain / complaint** *v./n.* 抱怨；控诉

### Page 368–369

**robust** *adj.* 强健的；强有力的
- 例：…who offered an even more **robust** defence of state privileges…

**complicated / complex / complexity** 复杂的；复杂性
- 例：…because of **complicated** family lives…

**clarity** *n.* 清晰（度）
- 例：…where conditions allow telescopes to obtain images of unsurpassed **clarity**.

### Page 370–375

**giant** *adj./n.* 巨大的；巨人
- a giant observatory 巨大的天文台

**internal** *adj.* 内部的；国内的
- Annals of Internal Medicine 内科医学年鉴
- 例：…focused on disciplinary problems and **internal** scholarly debates…

**squeeze** *v.* 压榨；挤入
- 例：…with the middle class disproportionately **squeezed**.

**fluctuate / fluctuation** *v./n.* 波动；起伏
- 例：…intelligence can expand and **fluctuate** according to mental effort.

**power / powerful** *n./adj.* 权力；强有力的
- 例：…driven by **powerful** and diverse motivations…

**conception / concept / conceive** 概念；观念；设想
- 例：…each other's **conceptions** of reason.
